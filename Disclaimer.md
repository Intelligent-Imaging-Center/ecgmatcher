# ECGMatcher Disclaimer

## 1\. Nature of the Software

ECGMatcher is a research software tool designed for electrocardiographic signal reconstruction, curve-oriented semantic segmentation, multi-lead morphological matching, and abnormality screening. The software is intended for scientific research, education, algorithm validation, and non-commercial evaluation. It is not a medical device approved or cleared by any regulatory authority.

## 2\. Not Intended for Clinical Diagnosis

The abnormality scores, similarity measures, most abnormal lead, key waveform segments, localized time intervals, heatmaps, segmentation masks, and clinical-support prompts generated by ECGMatcher:

* do not constitute a medical diagnosis;
* do not constitute medical advice;
* must not be used as a basis for emergency medical management;
* must not serve as the sole basis for medication selection or treatment planning;
* must not replace review of the original electrocardiogram and relevant clinical information by a licensed healthcare professional.

All outputs must be interpreted and independently verified by appropriately qualified professionals in conjunction with the original ECG, the patient's symptoms, medical history, laboratory findings, and other relevant clinical evidence.

## 3\. Model and Input Limitations

Software performance may be affected by, but is not limited to, the following factors:

* incorrect CSV formatting, sampling frequency, or temporal scaling;
* missing leads, incorrect lead mapping, or inconsistent lead ordering;
* baseline drift, noise, image compression artifacts, or discontinuous waveform traces;
* differences in acquisition devices, recording layouts, and amplitude scaling between the standard template and patient data;
* distribution shifts across populations, devices, disease categories, and acquisition settings;
* differences in model weights, software versions, and parameter configurations;
* variations in CPU, GPU, device drivers, and third-party runtime libraries.

Accordingly, a low abnormality score does not exclude the presence of a clinically significant abnormality, and a high abnormality score does not necessarily indicate any specific disease.

## 4\. Scope of Reported Research Performance

The performance reported in the repository and associated manuscript was obtained under specific datasets, input protocols, templates, and experimental settings. These results should not be interpreted as evidence that the software will achieve equivalent performance across all hospitals, devices, patient populations, or clinical environments.

## 5\. Data Security

ECGMatcher is designed to process electrocardiographic data and generate outputs locally. Nevertheless, users are responsible for:

* de-identifying data before processing or sharing;
* complying with applicable ethical-review requirements, informed-consent obligations, and institutional data-governance policies;
* refraining from uploading patient identifiers, original medical records, or unauthorized data to public repositories;
* reviewing output files, logs, case names, and screenshots for potentially identifiable information before disclosure.
